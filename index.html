When to buy

Buy when Bitcoin is cheap against its 200-week average

Bitcoin's major lows have landed at or just under its 200-week average: December 2018 (right on it), March 2020, 2022–23 and summer 2026. The further price stretches above it, the worse the next year has tended to be.

The rules

Three rules, checked once a week

  1. 01 · Trend

    Hold while above the 150-day average

    Bitcoin's big moves happen in long trends. Stay in while it's rising.

  2. 02 · Value

    Buy below the 200-week average

    Even in a downtrend, buy when price drops under its long-term floor. That's when it's been cheapest.

  3. 03 · Cash

    Otherwise, sit in cash

    Downtrend and not cheap yet: that's where the −70% to −80% crashes lived. Wait it out.

All-in or all-out, about 2 buys and 2 sells a year. Fewer sales means fewer taxable events; holding a spot Bitcoin ETF inside an IRA avoids them entirely.
